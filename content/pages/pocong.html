Controls for the Demo: Hold and release to jump. Swipe left and right to change direction the pocong is facing.

Primary Role: Game Designer, Game Director

This is a game project for Fun Ken Pon, Karya Eka Nala’s game division. A Jump King clone for mobile, but with pocong as the character. The Pocong is unable to walk and can only jump, but his jumps wrap around the screen — if he jumps to the right side of the screen, he will appear on the left side! This game is intended for speedrunners and game streamers.

I am responsible for:

  • Pitching the concept
  • Core gameplay loop
  • Overall art style and narrative design
  • Various balancing on system mechanics, like the character’s jump angle and speed
  • Level design
  • Overseeing the development team
