Cute pet battler mobile game, with unique turn-based battle system and elaborate breeding mechanics.

Company: Nomina Games
Project Type: Commercial
Platform: Android
Genre: Pet Battler
Status: In Development

Play: Google Play Store

Primary Role: Game Designer

My first project at Nomina Games. Cute pet battling game inspired by Axie Infinity. To differentiate, we change the battle system to a “dango formation”: slimes line up and can be moved forward and backward a la Darkest Dungeon. A slime can have 1 of 10 elements and 6 parts per slime, with elaborate genetics and breeding system. You can also pet the slimes and feed them berries.

Game modes include PVE Adventure, and real-time PVP.

I am responsible for:

  • Core battle system
  • Level and encounter design
  • Character and skill design
  • Breeding system
  • Petting system
  • EXP curve
  • Player economics (earning, ROI)
  • Testing and balancing
  • Collaborating with other departments to ensure features are shipped the best they can be
