Visual novel. You fish fish by talking to them. Save the village by catching the legendary Namazu.

Company: Self-project
Project Type: Game Jam
Platform: Browser, Windows, Mac
Genre: Visual Novel
Status: Released

Play: itch.io

Primary Role: Project Lead, Programmer (Ren’Py)

Entry for Novejam 2023 and won 1st place. I have an idea about a visual novel + fishing game, and when a visual novel game jam was announced, I immediately joined. I scouted a writer and illustrator and began to work on the game. We hit it off and submitted the game without significant problems and no cut features.

I am responsible for:
