Browser-based football management game, based on Garuda Eleven IP.

Company: Nomina Games
Project Type: Commercial
Platform: Browser
Genre: Sports, Simulation
Status: Released

Play: Link

Primary Role: Game Designer

Nomina Games worked with Garuda Eleven Team to revamp the core gameplay of their game. From pure card-and-stats-based gameplay, we changed it to be more of a football simulation game a la Football Manager, with formation and tactics system, player attributes and stamina, foul and penalty system, and consumables.

I am responsible for:

  • Core gameplay research and design, including match rules, formation and tactics attributes system, consumables system
  • Testing and balancing
  • Collaborating with other departments to ensure features are shipped the best they can be
  • Monitoring progress and reporting it to the client
