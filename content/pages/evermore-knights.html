Anime-inspired turn-based mobile RPG, with randomized character generation and elemental reaction system.

Company: Nomina Games
Project Type: Commercial
Platform: Android
Genre: RPG
Status: Open Beta

Website: Link
Play: Google Play Store

Primary Role: Game Designer

Nomina Games’s tentpole project. An anime-inspired turn-based RPG similar to Epic Seven. In Adventure mode you go through an epic story campaign to free Temeris from the influence of the the Dread, and in Arena mode you can battle with other player’s teams. Other features include Daily Dungeon, Dark Spire (Endless Tower), Crafting, Awakening, Battle Pass, Daily and Weekly Mission.

I am responsible for:

  • Level and encounter design
  • Character and skill design
  • Damage formula
  • EXP curve for characters and weapons
  • Elemental effectiveness chart and elemental reaction system
  • Player economics (earning, ROI)
  • Level design
  • Testing and balancing
  • Collaborating with other departments to ensure features are shipped the best they can be
