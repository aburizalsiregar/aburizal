A short, chaotic, and fast-paced typing game about cooking & serving dishes in an Italian restaurant on a busy day.

Company: Self-project
Project Type: Game Jam
Platform: Windows
Genre: Typing game
Status: Released

Play: itch.io

Primary Role: Programmer (Unity)

Entry for Game Jam Plus 2022 Indonesia, a 2-day game jam. The concept is Diner Dash combined with a typing game. So you must cook and serve dishes to customers, all with typing words. The game jam version won several awards and was incubated for 2 months. Unfortunately, I parted ways with the team after the jam. The refined version wound up winning the ‘Best Game Asia’ on GameJam+ along with 10 other awards.

I am responsible for:

  • Programming
  • Testing and balancing
