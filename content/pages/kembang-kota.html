Genre: Board Game
Player Size: 3-5 Players
Play Time: 30 Minutes
Status: Prototype

Rulebook: Click Here (Bahasa Indonesia)

Primary Role: Game Designer

A competitive party game, inspired by Kill Doctor Lucky, where you must confess to a girl while sabotaging your rivals’ confession attempt. Each round you move to an area, then you may battle a rival or make a confession attempt. You must be alone with the girl to confess. When you confess, you play a card. Each rivals then play any number or cards to sabotage your confession. You then roll a die to influence your final result. If the result is more than 0, then the confession is a success and you win the game.
