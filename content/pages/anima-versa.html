A simple and quick dueling card game with mechanics based on rock-paper-scissors and Old Maid.

Genre: Card Game
Player Size: 2 Players
Play Time: 5-15 Minutes
Status: Released (Self-Publish)

Primary Role: Game Producer, Game Designer

In this one-on-one dueling game, you pick two characters and battle your opponent. Each round, players pick a card and compare it based on rock-paper-scissors. The winner then guesses which card in the opponent’s hand is a Heart card. If correct, the opponent loses a Heart. Break two of the opponent’s Hearts to win.

Released in September 2022, the game started with 8 unique characters. Now, it is in its second season, which adds 4 more characters and streamlined rules. The game has been exhibited in various events like Comifuro, Jakarta Tabletop Expo, and GJUI.
